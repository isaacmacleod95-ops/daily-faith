Daily Faith Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Daily Faith creates original Bible stories, Christian motivational content and faith-based media. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when Daily Faith uses services such as TikTok and other social media platforms.

Information We May Process

When an authorized creator connects a social media account, the platform may provide basic account information and authorization credentials required to provide publishing features.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to operate Daily Faith, authenticate authorized accounts, and upload or manage Daily Faith content through supported platform APIs.

TikTok Integration

Daily Faith may use TikTok's official APIs to allow an authorized creator to connect their TikTok account and upload original Daily Faith videos to TikTok for review and publishing.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect account credentials and authorization tokens. Sensitive credentials are not intentionally published on this website or shared publicly.

Sharing of Information

Daily Faith does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to service providers such as TikTok when necessary to provide requested platform functionality.

Data Retention and Access

Authorization information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant functionality. Platform access can be revoked through the applicable platform account settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Daily Faith develops or its services change. Updates will be published on this page.
